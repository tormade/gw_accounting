## What to build

When a customer is selected in `Arbeiten`, switch from the two-task overview into a focused customer workplace. The return list should move out of the way, while customer data, warnings, recommended articles, last quantities, recent orders, and `Neue Bestellung aufnehmen` become the visible next step.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Selecting a customer focuses `Arbeiten` on that customer.
- [ ] The customer context shows address, delivery notes, payment method, and useful customer warnings.
- [ ] Open or overdue invoices for the selected customer are shown as a compact warning, not a competing work area.
- [ ] Recommended articles and last quantities are visible.
- [ ] Recent customer orders are visible as secondary context.
- [ ] `Neue Bestellung aufnehmen` is the primary customer action.
- [ ] UI tests cover the transition from start state to customer focus.
